Slab Leak Repair Houston

Slab leaks are a serious plumbing issue for Houston homeowners. If left untreated, they can cause foundation damage, mold growth, and high water bills.

What Is a Slab Leak?

A slab leak occurs when a water line beneath your home’s concrete foundation develops a leak due to corrosion, shifting soil, or aging pipes.

Signs You May Need Slab Leak Repair

  • Unexpected increase in water bills
  • Warm or damp spots on floors
  • Low water pressure
  • Sound of running water when fixtures are off
  • Foundation cracks
