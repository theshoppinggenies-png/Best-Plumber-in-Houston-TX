What Makes a Plumbing Problem an Emergency?

Understanding the difference between urgent situations requiring immediate response and issues that can wait until regular business hours helps you make informed decisions and prevents unnecessary emergency service charges.

Call for Emergency Service Immediately If:

  • You smell gas: Natural gas leaks create explosion and carbon monoxide poisoning risks. Evacuate and call immediately.
  • Water is actively flooding your home: Burst pipes, failed water heaters, or major leaks causing flooding require immediate intervention to prevent structural damage.
  • Sewage is backing up: Raw sewage in your home creates health hazards and requires urgent professional cleanup and repair.
  • You have no water at all: Complete loss of water service indicates main line problems requiring immediate diagnosis.
  • You hear water running constantly: Continuous water flow when all fixtures are off suggests major leaks wasting water and causing damage.
  • Your water heater is leaking extensively: Large amounts of water escaping from your water heater can flood homes with forty-plus gallons.
  • Frozen pipes have burst: During freezes, burst pipes release hundreds of gallons causing catastrophic damage if not stopped immediately.
  • Multiple drains are backing up: Whole-house drainage failures indicate main sewer line blockages requiring professional equipment.

These Can Usually Wait Until Regular Hours:

Not every plumbing issue requires emergency service. These situations, while inconvenient, don't typically pose immediate threats to your property or safety and can often be scheduled during regular business hours at lower cost: slow drains that still function, dripping faucets, running toilets that can be shut off at the valve, minor leaks you can contain with buckets, water pressure fluctuations, noisy pipes without leaks, and water heater problems when you still have some hot water available.

If you're unsure whether your situation qualifies as an emergency, call us anyway. Our experienced dispatchers can help you assess the severity and determine whether immediate service is necessary or if the problem can safely wait.

What to Do While Waiting for Emergency Plumber

The minutes between calling for help and our technician's arrival are critical for minimizing damage. These immediate actions can save thousands in property damage while you wait for professional assistance.

For Flooding and Burst Pipes:

  • Shut off the main water valve immediately: This stops water flow to your entire house. Every home should know where their main shutoff is located—usually near the water meter or where the main line enters your home.
  • Turn off electricity to affected areas: Water and electricity create dangerous situations. Shut off breakers to any rooms experiencing flooding.
  • Move valuables to dry areas: Quickly relocate furniture, electronics, documents, and other valuables away from flooding.
  • Start removing standing water: Use mops, towels, or a wet-dry vacuum to begin water removal and reduce damage to flooring and walls.
  • Document damage with photos: Take pictures of all damage for insurance claims before cleanup begins.
  • Open windows if weather permits: Increasing air circulation helps prevent mold growth and speeds drying.

For Gas Leaks:

  • Evacuate everyone immediately: Don't take time to gather belongings—get everyone outside to a safe distance.
  • Don't use any electrical switches or flames: Avoid turning lights on or off, using phones inside, or creating any sparks.
  • Leave doors and windows open: This allows gas to dissipate while you wait for emergency services.
  • Call from outside: Use a cell phone or neighbor's phone to call our emergency line and the gas company.
  • Don't return until cleared by professionals: Wait for our technicians and gas company representatives to declare it safe.

For Sewer Backups:

  • Stop using all water immediately: Don't flush toilets, run sinks, or use any drains until repairs are complete.
  • Avoid contact with sewage: Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria and pathogens. Keep people and pets away.
  • Ventilate the area: Open windows and doors to reduce fumes and odors.
  • Don't attempt to clean until after repairs: Wait for professional cleanup to avoid spreading contamination.
  • Identify any floor drains backing up: This helps our technician diagnose the problem location quickly.

Preventing Plumbing Emergencies

While our emergency service is always available when disasters strike, preventing emergencies in the first place saves money, stress, and property damage. These proactive measures reduce your risk of urgent plumbing failures.

Know Your Main Water Shutoff

Every household member should know where the main water shutoff valve is located and how to operate it. During burst pipe emergencies, shutting off the main valve immediately prevents thousands of gallons from flooding your home. Test the valve annually to ensure it operates properly and isn't frozen from corrosion or mineral buildup.

Schedule Annual Plumbing Inspections

Professional plumbing inspections identify potential failures before they become emergencies. Our technicians check for corroded pipes, deteriorating water heaters, tree root intrusion in sewer lines, and other issues that typically fail suddenly. Addressing these problems proactively during regular business hours costs far less than emergency repairs.

Don't Ignore Warning Signs

Most plumbing emergencies provide advance warning signs. Addressing these early indicators prevents full-blown crises. Watch for gradually increasing water bills suggesting hidden leaks, slow drains that worsen over time, water pressure changes, unusual sounds from pipes or water heaters, and small leaks that seem minor but indicate deteriorating components.

Protect Pipes During Freezes

Houston's occasional freezing weather causes numerous burst pipe emergencies. When temperatures drop near or below freezing, let faucets drip slightly to keep water moving, open cabinet doors under sinks to allow warm air circulation, disconnect and drain outdoor hoses, and insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas like garages and attics.

Regular Drain Maintenance

Clogged drains that backup into your home often develop gradually. Professional drain cleaning removes buildup before complete blockages occur. For homes with older sewer lines or trees near underground pipes, annual preventive cleaning prevents emergency backups. Our drain cleaning services keep systems flowing freely.
